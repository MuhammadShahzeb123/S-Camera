**Spec:** ../specs/2026-11-17-s-camera-design.md

## Global Constraints

Preserve launcher icon and GPL/attribution. All product work happens in S-Camera/camera. Retain Java namespace during parallel implementation; parent renames it after integration. Changes must not stop a recording for a rear lens switch or control edit. All unsupported controls use capability checks.

## Review Focus

Handoffs must reapply controls within new camera/route ranges. Manual shutter cannot exceed frame duration. Neutral grade must preserve colors and grade cannot affect alpha/handoff blending. Monitoring overlays cannot burn into video. Activity resume/rotation and unavailable capabilities cannot leave stale controls or stop buttons disabled.
